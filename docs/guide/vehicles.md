# Vehicles

**Household → Vehicles** keeps the household's cars and other vehicles in one place: their insurance, inspection and tax dates, their papers, and a service log.

## Add a vehicle

Choose **Add vehicle** and fill in what you know: a name the family uses ("Family car"), the plate, make, model, year, fuel, the chassis number (VIN), **Who drives it** and notes. Only the name is needed.

Under **What usually needs doing** tick what applies and give the next date for each:

| Duty | Repeats |
|---|---|
| Car insurance (liability) | every year |
| Comprehensive insurance | every year |
| Vehicle inspection | every year |
| Service | every year |
| Tyres | every six months |
| Vehicle tax or registration renewal | every year |

These are suggestions: how often a duty comes back differs by country and by family. Each one starts with a usual interval and reminder time, and you can change both when you tick it (every few months or years, or not at all).
You can add, change or remove duties later on the vehicle's page (see [What needs doing and Papers](records.md)), and add your own. Everything is tracked by **date**; there is no odometer. If you want to keep a mileage, write it in the notes of a service entry. Press **Edit** on the vehicle's page to change its details.

## Reminders

Each duty is a reminder in FamilyOS: you are told ahead of time (30 days for insurance and inspection, 21 for a service, 14 for tyres and tax; you can change it) and on the day. When it is done, tap **Done** and the date moves on by itself. For a renewed policy you can set the new date yourself in a suggestion (see below).

## Papers

Share a policy, an invoice or a registration card to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the vehicle. It is kept under **Papers** on the vehicle's page, and **the AI does not read it**. For something already in Quick add, use **File into…** on it.

## Service and repairs

The vehicle's page has **Service and repairs**, a log. Press **Add entry** and fill in the **Kind** (Service, Repair or Other), the **Date**, **Where** (garage or workshop) and **What was done**. You can link a paper (an invoice) to an entry with **Paper**. **Remove** (tap again to confirm) deletes an entry.

## What the AI does here

If you share a renewed policy or a service invoice without choosing **Add to…**, the AI sees your vehicles (name, plate, make, model, year and fuel; not the chassis number or notes) and the duties that are open, and suggests: mark the car insurance as done with the new end date, or add a service entry. You approve each suggestion before anything changes.

To keep vehicles away from the AI, set **Vehicles** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**. A vehicle's duties and papers go with it: the AI then sees nothing about your vehicles, not even a duty such as "Car insurance (Family car)". Everything keeps working by hand.

## Who can do what

Members who may edit vehicles change them directly; supervised members see the same buttons and forms, which say "After you save, someone has to approve this.", and their changes wait for approval. **Archive vehicle** (at the bottom of its page, tap again to confirm) hides the vehicle and stops its reminders; its papers and history are kept.
