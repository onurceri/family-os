# Vehicles

**Household → Vehicles**: cars and other vehicles with insurance, inspection and tax dates, papers and a service log.

## Add a vehicle

1. **Add vehicle.** Only the name is required; also: plate, make, model, year, fuel, chassis number (VIN), **Who drives it**, notes.
2. Under **What usually needs doing** tick duties and give the next date:

   | Duty | Repeats | Reminder |
   |---|---|---|
   | Car insurance (liability) | yearly | 30 days before |
   | Comprehensive insurance | yearly | 30 days before |
   | Vehicle inspection | yearly | 30 days before |
   | Service | yearly | 21 days before |
   | Tyres | every 6 months | 14 days before |
   | Vehicle tax or registration renewal | yearly | 14 days before |

**Good to know**
- Only suggestions: intervals differ by country and family. Change interval (or none) and reminder when you tick.
- Later, add, change or remove duties on the vehicle's page ([What needs doing and Papers](records.md)); **Edit** changes details.
- Dates only, no odometer: put mileage in a service entry's notes.
- You are told ahead and on the day. **Done** moves the date on. For a renewed policy you can set the new date in a suggestion.

## Papers

Share a policy, invoice or registration card to Quick add, **Add to…**, the vehicle (or **File into…**). It sits under **Papers**; **the AI does not read it**.

## Service and repairs

1. Vehicle page → **Service and repairs** → **Add entry**.
2. **Kind** (Service, Repair, Other), **Date**, **Where**, **What was done**; optionally an invoice via **Paper**.

**Remove** (tap again) deletes an entry.

## What the AI does here

For a renewed policy or service invoice shared without **Add to…**, the AI sees vehicles (name, plate, make, model, year, fuel; not VIN or notes) and open duties, and may suggest marking insurance done with the new end date or adding a service entry. You approve each.

To exclude vehicles, set **Vehicles** to **Off** in **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**; duties and papers go with it, so the AI sees no duty such as "Car insurance (Family car)". Manual use is unchanged.

## Who can do what

- Members who may edit vehicles change them directly.
- Supervised members get the same forms, marked "After you save, someone has to approve this."; their changes wait.
- **Archive vehicle** (page bottom, tap again) hides it and stops reminders; papers and history stay.
