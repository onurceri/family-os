# Vehicles

**Household → Vehicles** keeps the household's cars and other vehicles in one place: insurance, inspection and tax dates, papers, and a service log.

## Add a vehicle

1. Choose **Add vehicle** and fill in what you know: a name the family uses ("Family car"), plate, make, model, year, fuel, chassis number (VIN), **Who drives it** and notes. Only the name is needed.
2. Under **What usually needs doing** tick what applies and give the next date for each:

   | Duty | Repeats | Reminder |
   |---|---|---|
   | Car insurance (liability) | every year | 30 days before |
   | Comprehensive insurance | every year | 30 days before |
   | Vehicle inspection | every year | 30 days before |
   | Service | every year | 21 days before |
   | Tyres | every six months | 14 days before |
   | Vehicle tax or registration renewal | every year | 14 days before |

**Good to know**
- These are suggestions; how often a duty comes back differs by country and family. When you tick one, change the interval (every few months or years, or not at all) and the reminder time.
- Add, change or remove duties later on the vehicle's page, and add your own (see [What needs doing and Papers](records.md)). **Edit** on the page changes the vehicle's details.
- There is no odometer: dates only. To keep a mileage, write it in the notes of a service entry.
- You are told ahead of time and on the day. Tap **Done** and the date moves on by itself. For a renewed policy you can set the new date yourself in a suggestion (see below).

## Papers

Share a policy, an invoice or a registration card to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the vehicle (or **File into…** on something already in Quick add). It is kept under **Papers** on the vehicle's page, and **the AI does not read it**.

## Service and repairs

1. On the vehicle's page, in **Service and repairs**, press **Add entry**.
2. Fill in **Kind** (Service, Repair or Other), **Date**, **Where** (garage or workshop) and **What was done**.
3. Optionally link a paper (an invoice) with **Paper**.

**Remove** (tap again to confirm) deletes an entry.

## What the AI does here

If you share a renewed policy or a service invoice without choosing **Add to…**, the AI sees your vehicles (name, plate, make, model, year and fuel; not the chassis number or notes) and the open duties. It may suggest marking the car insurance done with the new end date, or adding a service entry. You approve each suggestion before anything changes.

To keep vehicles away from the AI, set **Vehicles** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**. Their duties and papers go with it: the AI sees nothing about your vehicles, not even a duty such as "Car insurance (Family car)". Everything keeps working by hand.

## Who can do what

- Members who may edit vehicles change them directly.
- Supervised members see the same buttons and forms, which say "After you save, someone has to approve this.", and their changes wait for approval.
- **Archive vehicle** (bottom of its page, tap again to confirm) hides the vehicle and stops its reminders; papers and history are kept.
