# Home

**Household → Home** keeps your homes in one place: their insurance and upkeep dates, the things in them with their warranties, and the utilities and subscriptions you pay for.

## Add a home

Choose **Add home** and give it a name the family uses ("Our flat"). The address, **Owner or tenant** (Owner, Tenant or Other) and notes are optional.

Under **What usually needs doing** tick what applies and give the next date for each:

| Duty | Repeats |
|---|---|
| Home insurance | every year |
| Heating or cooling service | every year |
| Smoke and CO alarms check | every year |
| Lease or contract review | every year |

These are suggestions: how often a duty comes back differs by country and by family. Each one starts with a usual interval and reminder time, and you can change both when you tick it (every few months or years, or not at all).
You can add, change or remove duties later on the home's page (see [What needs doing and Papers](records.md)), and add your own. Everything is tracked by **date**. Press **Edit** on the home's page to change its details.

## Things and warranties

On a home's page choose **Add thing** for an appliance or anything else worth keeping track of: a name ("Washing machine"), the **Room**, **Brand**, **Model**, **Serial number**, **Bought on** and **Warranty ends**.

If you give a warranty end date, FamilyOS adds a **Warranty ends** reminder under **What needs doing** on the thing's page, 30 days ahead. Change the date and the reminder moves; clear it and the reminder goes. If you mark the reminder done and the warranty is extended later, give the new date and a new reminder appears. When you add a thing you can also tick usual upkeep under **What usually needs doing**: **Filter change** (suggested every six months) and **Service** (suggested every year), each with an interval you can change.

## Utilities and subscriptions

On a home's page, **Subscriptions** lists what you pay for. Choose **Add service** and pick the **Kind** (Electricity, Gas, Water, Internet, Phone, TV, Insurance, Building dues or Other), then fill in the **Provider**, the **Account or subscriber number** and the **Usual billing day**. The billing day is only a note. Each one has its own page for papers (a contract, a bill) and reminders: add your own duty, for example when a contract ends.

## Papers

Share a receipt, a warranty card or a policy to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the home, the thing or the service. It is kept under **Papers** on its page, and **the AI does not read it**. For something already in Quick add, use **File into…** on it.

## What the AI does here

If you share a receipt or a warranty card without choosing **Add to…**, the AI sees your homes and things (home names, brand, model, room, warranty end; not addresses, serial numbers, account numbers or notes) and suggests: add the new thing to the right home with its purchase and warranty dates, or extend the warranty of one you already have. You approve each suggestion before anything changes. Utilities and subscriptions are never sent to the AI.

To keep your homes away from the AI, set **Home** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**. Their duties and papers go with it: the AI then sees nothing about your homes, not even a duty such as "Warranty ends (Washing machine)". Everything keeps working by hand.

## Who can do what

Members who may edit the home area change it directly; supervised members see the same buttons and forms, which say "After you save, someone has to approve this.", and their changes wait for approval. **Archive home**, **Archive thing** and **Archive service** (tap again to confirm) hide it and stop its reminders; archiving a home also hides its things and services. Papers and history are kept.
