# Home

**Household → Home** keeps your homes in one place: insurance and upkeep dates, the things in them with their warranties, and the utilities and subscriptions you pay for.

## Add a home

1. Choose **Add home** and give it a name the family uses ("Our flat"). The address, **Owner or tenant** (Owner, Tenant or Other) and notes are optional.
2. Under **What usually needs doing** tick what applies and give the next date for each:

   | Duty | Repeats |
   |---|---|
   | Home insurance | every year |
   | Heating or cooling service | every year |
   | Smoke and CO alarms check | every year |
   | Lease or contract review | every year |

**Good to know**
- These are suggestions; how often a duty comes back differs by country and family. When you tick one, change the interval (every few months or years, or not at all) and the reminder time.
- Add, change or remove duties later on the home's page, and add your own (see [What needs doing and Papers](records.md)). Everything is tracked by **date**. **Edit** on the page changes the home's details.

## Things and warranties

1. On a home's page choose **Add thing** for an appliance or anything else worth tracking.
2. Fill in a name ("Washing machine"), **Room**, **Brand**, **Model**, **Serial number**, **Bought on** and **Warranty ends**.
3. Optionally tick usual upkeep under **What usually needs doing**: **Filter change** (suggested every six months) and **Service** (suggested every year), each with an interval you can change.

**Good to know**
- A warranty end date adds a **Warranty ends** reminder under **What needs doing** on the thing's page, 30 days ahead.
- Change the date and the reminder moves; clear it and the reminder goes. If you marked it done and the warranty is extended later, give the new date and a new reminder appears.

## Utilities and subscriptions

1. On a home's page, in **Subscriptions**, choose **Add service**.
2. Pick the **Kind**: Electricity, Gas, Water, Internet, Phone, TV, Insurance, Building dues or Other.
3. Fill in the **Provider**, the **Account or subscriber number** and the **Usual billing day** (only a note).

Each one has its own page for papers (a contract, a bill) and reminders: add your own duty, for example when a contract ends.

## Papers

Share a receipt, a warranty card or a policy to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the home, the thing or the service (or **File into…** on something already in Quick add). It is kept under **Papers** on its page, and **the AI does not read it**.

## What the AI does here

If you share a receipt or a warranty card without choosing **Add to…**, the AI sees your homes and things (home names, brand, model, room, warranty end; not addresses, serial numbers, account numbers or notes). It may suggest adding the new thing to the right home with its purchase and warranty dates, or extending the warranty of one you have. You approve each suggestion before anything changes. Utilities and subscriptions are never sent to the AI.

To keep your homes away from the AI, set **Home** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**. Their duties and papers go with it: the AI sees nothing about your homes, not even a duty such as "Warranty ends (Washing machine)". Everything keeps working by hand.

## Who can do what

- Members who may edit the home area change it directly.
- Supervised members see the same buttons and forms, which say "After you save, someone has to approve this.", and their changes wait for approval.
- **Archive home**, **Archive thing** and **Archive service** (tap again to confirm) hide it and stop its reminders. Archiving a home also hides its things and services. Papers and history are kept.
