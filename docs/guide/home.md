# Home

**Household → Home**: homes with insurance and upkeep dates, things with warranties, and utilities and subscriptions.

## Add a home

1. **Add home** with a name ("Our flat"). Address, **Owner or tenant** (Owner, Tenant, Other) and notes are optional.
2. Under **What usually needs doing** tick duties and give the next date:

   | Duty | Repeats |
   |---|---|
   | Home insurance | yearly |
   | Heating or cooling service | yearly |
   | Smoke and CO alarms check | yearly |
   | Lease or contract review | yearly |

**Good to know**
- Only suggestions: intervals differ by country and family. Change interval (or none) and reminder when you tick.
- Later, add, change or remove duties on the home's page ([What needs doing and Papers](records.md)); dates only. **Edit** changes details.

## Things and warranties

1. Home page → **Add thing**.
2. Name ("Washing machine"), **Room**, **Brand**, **Model**, **Serial number**, **Bought on**, **Warranty ends**.
3. Optionally tick upkeep under **What usually needs doing**: **Filter change** (suggested every 6 months), **Service** (yearly); intervals editable.

**Good to know**
- A warranty end date adds **Warranty ends** under **What needs doing** on the thing's page, 30 days ahead.
- Changing the date moves it; clearing removes it. If you marked it done and the warranty is extended, enter the new date for a new reminder.

## Utilities and subscriptions

1. Home page → **Subscriptions** → **Add service**.
2. **Kind:** Electricity, Gas, Water, Internet, Phone, TV, Insurance, Building dues, Other.
3. **Provider**, **Account or subscriber number**, **Usual billing day** (a note only).

Each has its own page for papers (contract, bill) and reminders; add a duty, such as a contract end.

## Papers

Share a receipt, warranty card or policy to Quick add, **Add to…**, the home, thing or service (or **File into…**). It sits under **Papers**; **the AI does not read it**.

## What the AI does here

For a receipt or warranty card shared without **Add to…**, the AI sees homes and things (home names, brand, model, room, warranty end; not addresses, serial or account numbers, notes) and may suggest adding the thing with its dates or extending a warranty. You approve each. Utilities and subscriptions are never sent.

To exclude homes, set **Home** to **Off** in **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**; duties and papers go with it, so the AI sees no duty such as "Warranty ends (Washing machine)". Manual use is unchanged.

## Who can do what

- Members who may edit the home area change it directly.
- Supervised members get the same forms, marked "After you save, someone has to approve this."; their changes wait.
- **Archive home**, **Archive thing**, **Archive service** (tap again) hide it and stop reminders; archiving a home hides its things and services. Papers and history stay.
