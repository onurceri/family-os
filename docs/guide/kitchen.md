# Kitchen

**Household → Kitchen** keeps the family's recipes and a simple meal plan. It has two tabs: **Recipes** and **Meal plan**. There are no units or conversions; ingredients are just lines of text, the way you would write them on a note.

## Recipes

Choose **Add recipe** and fill in the **Name**, **Servings** (as you write it, such as 4 or 4–6), **Total time in minutes**, the **Ingredients** (one per line), the **Steps** (one per line), **Notes**, the **Source (web address)** if you like (it is only kept as a link; nothing is fetched from it) and **Labels** separated by commas. Only the name is needed. On a recipe's page you can **Edit** it, **Add photo** (a JPEG, PNG or WebP image under 8 MB) or **Archive recipe** (tap again to confirm). **Search recipes** and the label filter on the Recipes tab find a recipe again.

### Ingredients to the shopping list

On a recipe's page, tick the ingredients you need (**Select all** ticks every line) and choose **Add selected to shopping list**. They are added to the shopping list with the product as the name and the amount as the quantity; a preparation note after a comma ("Lemon, zest and juice") is left out of the name. Items already on the list are not added twice and are left as they are. A message and a short list under the ingredients say what was added, what was already there ("Chicken breast is already on the list (500 g; the recipe needs 600 g)") and what was not added. This needs Lists to be switched on; if you have several shopping lists you can choose which one.

## Meal plan

The **Meal plan** tab shows one week at a time. Each day shows **Breakfast**, **Lunch** and **Dinner**; tap an empty one to fill it. A panel lists your recipes first; pick one, or choose **Something else (write it)** and write what is cooked. The **+** on a day adds a meal to any slot, **Other** included. Several meals can share a slot, and the **×** beside a meal takes it off the plan. The arrows step through the weeks and **This week** comes back to now. Today's meals also appear on the Today screen.

### A recipe onto the plan

- On a recipe's page, press **Put it on the meal plan** beside its name.
- On the **Recipes** tab, each recipe has a calendar button that does the same.

Choose the **Day** and the **Meal** and press **Add**. If the same dish is already planned for that meal on that day, the panel says so and asks you to tick **Add it anyway**. A message says **Added to the meal plan**, with **See the plan**.

## What the AI does here

If you share a photo of a recipe, a page of text or a message with a recipe to Quick add, the AI suggests a recipe with the ingredients as separate lines, in the original language, and you approve it. It can also suggest a meal for a day ("pasta on Friday"). The assistant can answer "what is for dinner" and "what recipes do we have". Every change is a suggestion you approve.

To keep the kitchen away from the AI, set **Kitchen** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**.

## Who can do what

Members who may edit the kitchen change things directly; supervised members can look, and their changes to recipes and the meal plan wait for approval (only members who may edit can add a photo). Archiving a recipe hides it; meals on the plan that use it stay.
