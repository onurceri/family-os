# Region and formats

The **[language](languages.md)** chooses words (including day and month names). The **region format** chooses how dates, times, numbers, money and weeks are laid out: English words with day-first dates, a 24-hour clock and Monday weeks is fine.

## Set the region

1. Open **Settings → Family → Language & region** (permission to change family settings; admins have it).
2. Set:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Family name** | Optional ("The Novaks"). Shown on the sign-in page above the heading (family devices only), small on paired displays, and in Telegram's link message. Empty it to remove. |
| **Time zone** | Decides what "today" is and when reminders go off. |
| **Region format** | Search a country or region, or type a tag like `en-GB`. Sets day/month/year order, number marks (`1,234.56` or `1.234,56`) and money. **Use this browser's region** fills it in. An invalid tag cannot be saved. |
| **First day of the week** | Starts calendar and meal plan weeks, **This week** and **Next week** on Tasks, and the day pickers of repeating events, tasks and chores. Never changes the days an event repeats on. |
| **Clock** | 24-hour (14:30) or 12-hour (2:30 PM). |
| **Reminder time** | The time of day a reminder goes out for something that has a day but no time, such as a chore, a record's due date or an all-day event (09:00 until you change it). A time moved later also moves reminders already planned; an earlier time applies to reminders planned from then on. |
| **Temperature** | °C or °F, for the displays' weather. |

3. Check the **Example** line at the top (a date, a time, a number and a temperature). Changes are saved as you make them.

**Good to know**
- Changing the region format moves week start, clock and temperature to that region's usual; adjust before **Save**.
- Names stay in each reader's language: with Slovenia picked, English readers see "Thu, 22 Oct", Slovenian readers "čet., 22. okt.".
- Setup guesses time zone and region from the browser and shows them under **Time and region** before the account is created.
- Other open screens update by themselves when these settings change.

## Where it applies

- Every date, time, amount and number in the app and on displays.
- Notifications and Telegram messages, written when sent, with dates in each recipient's language.
- Quick add: "this week" and "next week" follow your first weekday, and the AI is told your date order and clock, so "03/04" is read your family's way. Proposed dates are stored in one fixed form. A numeric date that could be swapped is marked "Check this" when the AI read it the other way.
- The assistant gives times in your clock.
