# Region and formats

Language and region are two settings. The **[language](languages.md)** chooses the words (including day and month names). The **region format** chooses how dates, times, numbers, money and weeks are laid out. A family can read English and still have day-first dates, a 24-hour clock and weeks that start on Monday.

## Set the region

1. Open **Settings → Family → Language & region** (needs the permission to change family settings; admins have it).
2. Set what you need:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Family name** | Optional, for example "The Novaks". Shown above the sign-in page's heading on a family device only (a browser nobody trusted never sees it), small on paired family displays (above Today and beside the time on each page between Today's turns) and in the message Telegram sends when someone links their account. Empty it to remove the name. |
| **Time zone** | Search the list. Decides what "today" is and when reminders go off. |
| **Region format** | Search for your country or region, or type a tag such as `en-GB` or `de-DE`. Decides the order of day, month and year, the marks in numbers (`1,234.56` or `1.234,56`) and how money is written. **Use this browser's region** fills it in from your browser. A tag that is not valid cannot be saved. |
| **First day of the week** | Calendar and meal plan weeks start here; the Tasks page's **This week** and **Next week** follow it; the day pickers of repeating events, tasks and chores list days in that order. It never changes the days an event repeats on. |
| **Clock** | 24-hour (14:30) or 12-hour (2:30 PM). |
| **Temperature** | °C or °F, for the weather on the family display. |

3. Check the **Preview** (a date, a time, a number and a temperature) and press **Save**.

**Good to know**
- Changing the region format moves the week start, clock and temperature to what that region usually uses; you can still change each before **Save**.
- Day and month names stay in each person's language: with Slovenia picked, someone who reads English sees "Thu, 22 Oct" and someone who reads Slovenian "čet., 22. okt.".
- Other open screens update by themselves.
- At setup, the time zone and region format are guessed from your browser and shown under **Time and region**, with a preview, before the account is created. Correct them there or later here.

## Where it applies

- Every date, time, amount of money and number in the app and on the family display.
- Reminder and notification texts, written when sent, with dates in each recipient's own language. Telegram messages too.
- Quick add: "this week" and "next week" in the dates the AI sees follow your first day of the week, and the AI is told your date order and clock, so "03/04" is read the way your family writes it. Proposed dates are always stored in one fixed form; only what you see changes. A date written only in numbers, where day and month could be swapped, is marked "Check this" when the AI read it the other way.
- The assistant tells you times in your clock.
