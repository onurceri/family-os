# Region and formats

Language and region are two separate settings. The **[language](languages.md)** (English, Turkish or any installed pack) chooses the words in FamilyOS. The **region format** chooses how dates, times, numbers, money and weeks look. A family can read English and still have day-first dates, a 24-hour clock and weeks that start on Monday.

## What you can set

Open **Settings → Family → Language & region** (you need the permission to change family settings, which admins have).

- **Time zone.** Search the list and pick the family's zone; it decides what "today" is and when reminders go off. Other open screens update by themselves when any of these settings change.
- **Region format.** A language tag such as `en-GB`, `en-US` or `de-DE`. It decides the order of day, month and year, the marks in numbers (`1,234.56` or `1.234,56`) and how money is written. Type a tag or pick one from the suggestions; the button **Use this browser's region** fills it in from your browser. A tag that is not valid cannot be saved.
- **First day of the week.** Calendar and meal plan weeks start here, and the day pickers of repeating events, tasks and chores list the days in that order. Changing it never changes the days an event repeats on.
- **Clock.** 24-hour (14:30) or 12-hour (2:30 PM).
- **Temperature.** °C or °F, for the weather on the family display.

When you change the region format, the week start, clock and temperature jump to what that region usually uses. You can still change each of them before you press **Save**. The **Preview** shows a date, a time, a number and a temperature the way they will look.

## Where it applies

- Every date, time, amount of money and number in the app and on the family display.
- Reminder texts, which are written when they are sent.
- Quick add: "this week" and "next week" in the dates the AI sees follow your first day of the week, and the AI is told your date order and clock, so "03/04" is read the way your family writes it. The dates it proposes are always stored in one fixed form; only what you see changes. A date written only in numbers, where day and month could be swapped, is marked "Check this" when the AI read it the other way.
- The assistant tells you times in your clock.

## At setup

Setup guesses the time zone and the region format from your browser and shows both under **Time and region**, with what dates and times will look like, before the account is created. Correct a wrong guess there; you can change them later here.
