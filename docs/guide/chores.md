# Chores

**Household → Chores** keeps the household's shared, rotating work: who is up for the dishes, what is due today, what is late. A one-person to-do with a deadline ("sign the school form") belongs on the Lists tab as a task.

## Add a chore

Choose **Add chore** and fill in:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chore**, **Notes** | Notes are optional. |
| **First day it is due** | The first date. |
| **Repeats** | *Once*, *Every day*, *Every week* (on the days you pick), or *Every month*, with an optional **Until** date. |
| **Who takes turns** | Tap the people. With more than one, an order list appears: move people up or down; the first is up first. Empty means anyone can do it. |
| **How the turn moves on** | Shown with more than one person (*Take turns* at first; with one person it is always that person). |

**How the turn moves on**
- *Always the same person*: the first in the list.
- *Take turns*: after someone does it, the next in the list is up.
- *Whoever did it least*: the person who did it fewest times in the last 90 days (ties go to the one listed first).

**Good to know**
- On a new weekly chore the day of the first date is chosen for you and follows it when you change the date; a line under the days says the next day it falls on.
- A chore due on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.
- Moving the date or days of a saved repeating chore asks **Only this one** (this turn moves, the repeat stays) or **This one and the ones after** (the repeat changes from now on; turns already done are kept). Tasks and calendar events ask the same.
- The page groups chores into **Overdue**, **Today**, **Coming up** and **Done** (finished one-off chores). **Everyone** and **Mine** switch the view; **Mine** shows your turns and chores anyone can do.
- Tap a chore's name to open it, change it or **Archive** it (tap again to confirm).
- People who cannot edit chores see **Suggest a chore** instead of **Add chore**; the form says "After you save, someone has to approve this." and their change waits for approval.
- If you ticked a chore off yourself today, opening it says "You marked this done today." with **Undo**, so a tick can be taken back after the notice is gone.

## Doing a chore

Tap the circle in front of a chore. The next due date is set, the turn passes on, and a notice offers **Undo**.

**Good to know**
- You can undo your own tick the same day; someone who can edit chores can undo any.
- Double taps do not count twice.
- A chore can be ticked ahead of its day (a weekly one a day early); the notice names the day it counted for, such as **Walk the dog done for tomorrow**.
- If a chore was missed for a while, the next date is after today, so chores never pile up behind a holiday.
- A chore that does not repeat moves to **Done** and shows who did it.
- You can tick off your own turn and chores anyone may do. Members who can edit chores can tick off any chore; it counts for the person whose turn it was, so turns and "least done" stay fair. A chore anyone may do counts for the person who taps.
- The **My chores today** card on Today lists your due chores and lets you tick them off in place.

## Reminders

On the day a chore is due, at 09:00, the person whose turn it is gets a notification. Chores anyone can do remind nobody. Turn it off per person under notification preferences.

## What the AI does here

The assistant answers "whose turn is the dishes?" and "what do I have this week?". It can suggest chores, or a fair split of the existing ones, as suggestions in Quick add that you approve (each can be taken back). It never ticks chores off. To keep chores away from the AI, set **Chores** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**.

## Who can do what

Access is by capability, not role name. By default:

| | See chores | Tick off | Add, change, assign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin | yes | yes | yes |
| Member | yes | yes | yes |
| Supervised | yes | yes | suggestions wait for approval |
| Guest | yes | no | no |

A family can grant any of these to a custom role under **Settings → Family → Roles**.
