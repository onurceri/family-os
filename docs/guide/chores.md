# Chores

**Household → Chores** keeps the household's shared work: who is up for the dishes, what is due today, what is late. Chores take turns fairly and are ticked off with one tap.

Chores are for shared, rotating housework. A one-person to-do with a deadline ("sign the school form") belongs on the Lists tab as a task.

## Add a chore

Choose **Add chore** and fill in:

- **Chore** and optional **Notes**.
- **First day it is due**, and how often it **Repeats**: *Once*, *Every day*, *Every week* (on the days you pick; on a new chore the day of the first date is chosen for you and follows it when you change the date; a line under the days says the next day it falls on) or *Every month*, with an optional **Until** date. A chore due on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months. When you move the date or the days of a saved repeating chore, saving asks **Only this one** (this turn moves, the repeat stays) or **This one and the ones after** (the repeat changes from now on; turns already done are kept). Tasks and calendar events ask the same.
- **Who takes turns**: tap the people; with more than one, the order list appears and you move people up or down. The first one is up first. Leave it empty and anyone can do it.
- **How the turn moves on**, when more than one person is listed (*Take turns* is chosen at first; with one person it is always that person):
  - *Always the same person*: the first person in the list.
  - *Take turns*: after someone does it, the next person in the list is up.
  - *Whoever did it least*: the person who did it the fewest times in the last 90 days is up (ties go to the one listed first).

The page groups chores into **Overdue**, **Today**, **Coming up** and **Done** (one-off chores that are finished). **Everyone** and **Mine** switch the view: **Mine** shows only your turns and the chores anyone can do. Tap a chore's name to open it, change it or **Archive** it (tap again to confirm). People who cannot edit chores see **Suggest a chore** instead of **Add chore**; when they open a chore the form says "After you save, someone has to approve this.", and their change waits for approval. If you ticked the chore off yourself today, the opened chore says "You marked this done today." with **Undo**, so a tick can be taken back after the notice is gone.

## Doing a chore

Tap the circle in front of a chore. The next due date is set, the turn passes on, and a notice offers **Undo** (you can undo your own tick the same day; someone who can edit chores can undo any). Double taps do not count twice. A chore can be ticked off ahead of its day (a weekly one a day early); the notice then names the day it counted for, such as **Walk the dog done for tomorrow**. If a chore was missed for a while, the next date is after today, so chores never pile up behind a holiday. A chore that does not repeat moves to **Done** and shows who did it.

You can tick off your own turn and chores anyone may do. Members who can edit chores can tick off any chore, and it is counted as done by the person whose turn it was, so turns and "least done" stay fair. A chore anyone may do is counted for the person who taps.

The **My chores today** card on the Today screen lists your chores that are due and lets you tick them off in place.

## Reminders

On the day a chore is due, at 09:00, the person whose turn it is gets a notification. Chores anyone can do remind nobody. Turn it off per person under notification preferences.

## What the AI does here

The assistant can answer "whose turn is the dishes?" and "what do I have this week?". It can also suggest chores, or a fair split of the existing ones between the family, as suggestions in Quick add that you approve (each can be taken back). It never ticks chores off. To keep chores away from the AI, set **Chores** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**.

## Who can do what

Access is by capability, not by role name. By default:

| | See chores | Tick off | Add, change, assign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin | yes | yes | yes |
| Member | yes | yes | yes |
| Supervised | yes | yes | suggestions wait for approval |
| Guest | yes | no | no |

A family can grant any of these to a custom role under **Settings → Family → Roles**.
