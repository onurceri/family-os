# Chores

**Household → Chores**: shared, rotating housework: whose turn, what is due, what is late. A one-person to-do with a deadline belongs on Lists as a task.

## Add a chore

**Add chore**, then:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Chore**, **Notes** | Notes optional. |
| **First day it is due** | First date. |
| **Repeats** | *Once*, *Every day*, *Every week* (days you pick), *Every month*; optional **Until**. |
| **Who takes turns** | Tap people; with several, reorder them (first is up first). Empty = anyone. |
| **How the turn moves on** | With several people (starts at *Take turns*; one person is always that person). |

**How the turn moves on**
- *Always the same person*: first in the list.
- *Take turns*: next in the list after each completion.
- *Whoever did it least*: fewest completions in the last 90 days (ties: first listed).

**Good to know**
- New weekly chores pick the first date's weekday and follow date changes; a line under the days names the next date.
- A chore due on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.
- Moving the date or days of a saved repeating chore asks **Only this one** (this turn moves, repeat stays) or **This one and the ones after** (repeat changes from now; done turns kept). Tasks and events ask the same.
- Groups: **Overdue**, **Today**, **Coming up**, **Done** (finished one-offs). **Mine** shows your turns and anyone-may-do chores; **Everyone** shows all.
- Tap a name to open, edit or **Archive** (tap again).
- Without edit rights you see **Suggest a chore**; the form says "After you save, someone has to approve this." and the change waits.
- A chore you ticked today shows "You marked this done today." with **Undo**.

## Doing a chore

Tap its circle. The next date is set, the turn passes on, and a notice offers **Undo**.

**Good to know**
- Undo your own tick the same day; editors can undo any.
- Double taps count once.
- Ticking ahead is allowed (a weekly one a day early); the notice names the day, like **Walk the dog done for tomorrow**.
- After a long miss, the next date is after today: chores never pile up behind a holiday.
- A non-repeating chore moves to **Done** with who did it.
- You tick your own turns and anyone-may-do chores. Editors tick any chore, counted for the person whose turn it was (fair turns and "least done"); anyone-may-do counts for the tapper.
- **My chores today** on Today lists your due chores to tick in place.

## Reminders

At 09:00 on the due day, the person whose turn it is gets a notification. Anyone-may-do chores remind nobody. Turn off per person in notification preferences.

## What the AI does here

The assistant answers "whose turn is the dishes?" and "what do I have this week?", and can suggest chores or a fair split in Quick add for you to approve (undoable). It never ticks chores. To exclude chores, set **Chores** to **Off** in **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**.

## Who can do what

By capability, not role name. Defaults:

| | See chores | Tick off | Add, change, assign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin | yes | yes | yes |
| Member | yes | yes | yes |
| Supervised | yes | yes | suggestions wait for approval |
| Guest | yes | no | no |

Custom roles: **Settings → Family → Roles**.
