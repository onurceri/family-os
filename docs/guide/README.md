# FamilyOS user guide

For whoever runs FamilyOS at home. Start with the first page; the others can wait until you need them.

1. [Install](install.md): Docker, first setup, adding the family.
2. [HTTPS and phones](https.md): reach FamilyOS from phones, install the app, share to it.
3. [Everyday use](everyday.md): Today, Quick add, the calendar, shopping and tasks.
4. [Family members, roles and devices](members.md): add people, choose what each may do, and set up phones and tablets.
5. [Backups](backups.md): what is backed up, copying backups away, restoring.
6. [AI and privacy](ai.md): choosing a provider, what the AI sees, turning things off.
7. [Notifications](notifications.md): reminders, suggestions to review, phone notifications, quiet hours.
8. [Telegram](telegram.md): share to Quick add and approve from Telegram.
9. [Email](email.md): school notices and other mail in Quick add, from a dedicated mailbox.
10. [Calendars](calendars.md): the calendar on your phone, school and Google calendars, reminders.
11. [Family displays](displays.md): a wall tablet with today's plans, photos and weather.
12. [What needs doing and Papers](records.md): duties that come back, reminders, filing papers, archiving.
13. [Vehicles](vehicles.md): insurance and inspection dates, papers and a service log.
14. [Home](home.md): insurance, upkeep dates, things with warranties, and utilities.
15. [Pets](pets.md): vaccination and parasite dates, vet details, papers and a treatment history.
16. [Chores](chores.md): rotating household chores with one-tap done.
17. [The assistant](assistant.md): ask in plain language, suggestions to approve, what it remembers, what it sees, turning it off.
18. [Auto-approval](auto-approval.md): let routine suggestions, such as shopping items, be approved by themselves, safely and visibly.
19. [Bring your own agent (MCP)](mcp.md): connect your own AI agent, such as Hermes Agent or Claude Desktop, with a personal token; it reads what you may see and its changes are suggestions you approve.
20. [Kitchen](kitchen.md): recipes, a weekly meal plan and ingredients to the shopping list.
21. [The family board](board.md): short notes on paper for everyone, with a photo, an end day and a pin; on Today and on the family display.
22. [Using it offline](offline.md): shopping, tasks and chores keep working without a connection and are sent later.
23. [Search](search.md): find recipes, home things, events, duties and shared items by meaning, for you and for the assistant.
24. [Region and formats](region.md): date, time, number and week formats, separate from the language.
25. [Languages](languages.md): choose the language of the app and of each person, add or remove language packs, make your own.
26. [Appearance](appearance.md): choose a theme for the family, for yourself and for a display, add or remove themes, make your own.
27. [Extensions](extensions.md): install the kitchen, pets, vehicles or home, see what each reaches, switch one off or uninstall it.
