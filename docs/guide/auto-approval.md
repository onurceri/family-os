# Auto-approval

Applies routine AI suggestions without waiting. Admins only: **Settings → AI → Auto-approval**. It covers suggestions from Quick add, the [assistant](assistant.md) and connected agents ([MCP](mcp.md)).

1. Open **Settings → AI → Auto-approval**.
2. Turn on the switch for a kind.
3. Under **Apply the AI's suggestions when they are for someone who can**, pick the right.

| Kind | Right the suggestion's person must hold |
|---|---|
| Shopping items | **Create, rename and remove shopping lists**, or more widely **Add and tick off shopping items** |
| Tasks | **Add and change tasks for anyone** |

**Good to know**
- Only these two kinds exist. Everything else, and suggestions for anyone else, waits for a person.
- Off by default: a forwarded message may hide instructions for the AI. Use it for routine you trust.
- It approves **as the person who turned it on**, under the usual approval rules.
- A suggestion for one person alone (an assistant note to remember) is always theirs to decide. People, roles and settings never qualify.
- Approved suggestions stay in Quick add, marked **Approved automatically**, with **Undo** (also from the assistant's answer).
- It stops by itself, with the notification **Auto-approval stopped**, if the person who turned it on is archived or loses the right to manage it or approve that kind. The notification goes to them, or to the other admins if they are gone. Turn it on again to continue (it then approves as you).
