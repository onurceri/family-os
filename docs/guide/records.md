# What needs doing and Papers

Vehicles, pets and homes share two sections, **What needs doing** and **Papers**. There is no separate Records page: open [Vehicles](vehicles.md), [Pets](pets.md) or [Home](home.md) from **Household** in the side bar (**More → Household** on a phone).

## What needs doing

A duty is something that has to happen by a date: renew the car insurance, vaccinate Luna, service the heating, a warranty that ends. Each has a title, a **due date** and, if it comes back, how often. Everything is tracked by **date**; there is no odometer or other counter.

1. Press **Add** to write your own duty, or tap one under **What usually needs doing** when you add a vehicle, pet or home.
2. Set **Repeats**: *Does not repeat*, *Every … months* or *Every … years*, with a number. Choosing one starts at 3 months or 2 years; change it.
3. Optionally set **Who**: *Anyone*, or the person who looks after it.
4. When it is done press **Done**, optionally write a note ("new policy number") and press **Save as done**.

**Good to know**
- A repeating duty is counted from its **first due date**, never from the day you did it. A duty on the 31st falls on the last day of a shorter month and comes back to the 31st (31 January, 28 February, 31 March); one on 29 February falls on 28 February in other years.
- After **Done**, a repeating duty moves to its next date and the old one goes to **History** (press **History** on the duty). If you were late, the next date is the first one after today. A duty that does not repeat is finished and listed under "finished".
- **Edit** changes a duty. **Remove** (tap again to confirm) deletes it.
- A late duty shows **Overdue**; one inside its reminder window shows **Due soon**.
- **Coming up** on Today lists duties due in the next 30 days or overdue (five at a time, then "and N more"). A wall display can show the same list if you gave it that (see [Family displays](displays.md)).

### Reminders

You are told **Remind me this many days before** the due date and again on the due day at 09:00. Each suggested duty has its own lead time, usually two to four weeks; a duty you add yourself has 14.

**Good to know**
- If you chose **Who**, only that person hears about it; otherwise everyone who may see these records does. See [Notifications](notifications.md).
- When a repeating duty is done, the reminders move to its next date. Archiving the record or removing the duty stops them.

## Papers

**Papers** are files that belong to a vehicle, pet, home, thing or service: a policy, an invoice, a vet card, a warranty.

1. Share the file to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the record. For something already in Quick add, use **File into…** on it.
2. Choose a **Kind of paper**: Policy, Invoice, Card, Receipt or Other.

**Good to know**
- **Edit** on a paper changes the kind or sets **Valid until** if it runs out. Expired papers are marked.
- **Papers filed by hand with Add to… are never sent to the AI.** It does not read them and makes no suggestion. This keeps a sensitive document at home without turning anything off.
- **Detach** removes a paper from the record; it stays in Quick add.

## Archiving

Archiving a vehicle, pet or home hides it, stops its reminders and keeps its papers and history. Duties you no longer need can be removed on their own with **Remove** (tap again to confirm).

## And the AI?

If you share a document without **Add to…**, the AI may suggest marking a duty done or adding an entry; you approve every suggestion. You decide per area how far it may look, and a locked area hides its duties and papers from it: see [Keeping areas away from the AI](ai.md#keeping-areas-away-from-the-ai).
