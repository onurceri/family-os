# What needs doing and Papers

Vehicles, pets and homes share **What needs doing** and **Papers**. No separate Records page: open [Vehicles](vehicles.md), [Pets](pets.md) or [Home](home.md) from **Household** in the side bar (**More → Household** on a phone).

## What needs doing

A duty must happen by a date (renew insurance, vaccinate Luna, service the heating, warranty ends): title, **due date**, and how often it returns. Dates only; no odometer or counter.

1. **Add** your own, or tap one under **What usually needs doing** when adding a vehicle, pet or home.
2. **Repeats:** *Does not repeat*, *Every … months* or *Every … years*, with a number (starts at 3 months or 2 years).
3. Optionally **Who**: *Anyone* or one person.
4. When done: **Done**, optional note ("new policy number"), **Save as done**.

**Good to know**
- Repeats count from the **first due date**, not from when you did it. The 31st falls on a shorter month's last day and returns to the 31st (31 Jan, 28 Feb, 31 Mar); 29 Feb falls on 28 Feb in other years.
- A repeating duty moves to its next date; the old one goes to **History**. If late, the next date is the first after today. A non-repeating duty is finished and listed under "finished".
- **Edit** changes it; **Remove** (tap again) deletes it.
- **Overdue** when late; **Due soon** inside the reminder window.
- Today's **Coming up** lists duties due within 30 days or overdue (five, then "and N more"). A wall display can show it too ([Family displays](displays.md)).

### Reminders

**Remind me this many days before** the due date, and again on the due day, both at the family's [reminder time](region.md) (09:00 unless changed). Suggested duties have their own lead (usually 2–4 weeks); your own default to 14.

**Good to know**
- With **Who**, only that person is told; otherwise everyone who may see these records ([Notifications](notifications.md)).
- Reminders follow a repeating duty to its next date. Archiving the record or removing the duty stops them.

## Papers

Files for a vehicle, pet, home, thing or service: policy, invoice, vet card, warranty.

1. Share to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the record. For something already in Quick add: **File into…**.
2. Pick a **Kind of paper**: Policy, Invoice, Card, Receipt, Other.

**Good to know**
- **Edit** changes the kind or sets **Valid until**; expired papers are marked.
- **Papers filed with Add to… are never sent to the AI**: not read, no suggestion. A way to keep a sensitive document at home without turning anything off.
- **Detach** removes it from the record; it stays in Quick add.

## Archiving

A duty or paper of a vehicle, pet or home is shown only to people who may see that area: someone without access to pets sees no pet duty or paper in lists, "Coming up" or search.

Archiving a vehicle, pet or home hides it and stops its reminders; papers and history stay. Unneeded duties: **Remove** (tap again).

## And the AI?

Documents shared without **Add to…** may get suggestions (mark a duty done, add an entry); you approve each. Locked areas hide their duties and papers from it: [Keeping areas away from the AI](ai.md#keeping-areas-away-from-the-ai).
