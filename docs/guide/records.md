# What needs doing and Papers

Vehicles, pets and homes share two sections on their pages, **What needs doing** and **Papers**. They work the same everywhere, so learn them once. (There is no separate Records page: open [Vehicles](vehicles.md), [Pets](pets.md) or [Home](home.md) from **Household** in the side bar (**More → Household** on a phone).)

## What needs doing

A duty is something that has to happen by a date: renew the car insurance, vaccinate Luna, service the heating, a warranty that ends. Each has a title, a **due date** and, if it comes back, how often.

- Press **Add** to write your own duty, or tap one under **What usually needs doing** when you add a vehicle, pet or home. Everything is tracked by **date**; there is no odometer or other counter.
- **Repeats** is *Does not repeat*, *Every … months* or *Every … years*, with a number (every 3 months, every 2 years); choosing one starts at 3 months or 2 years, which you can change. A repeating duty is always counted from its **first due date**, never from the day you did it. So a duty on the 31st falls on the last day of a shorter month and comes back to the 31st (31 January, 28 February, 31 March), and one on 29 February falls on 28 February in other years.
- **Who** is optional. Leave it as *Anyone*, or pick the person who looks after it.
- Press **Done** when it is done. You can write a note ("new policy number") and press **Save as done**. A repeating duty moves to its next date by itself and the old one goes to **History** (press **History** on the duty). If you were late, the next date is the first one after today. A duty that does not repeat is finished and listed under "finished".
- **Edit** changes a duty. **Remove** (tap again to confirm) deletes it.
- A duty that is late shows **Overdue**; one inside its reminder window (see below) shows **Due soon**. **Coming up** on Today lists the duties that are due in the next 30 days or overdue (five at a time, then "and N more"), and a wall display can show the same list if you gave it that (see [Family displays](displays.md)).

### Reminders

You are told **Remind me this many days before** the due date (each suggested duty has its own, usually two to four weeks; 14 for one you add yourself) and again on the due day at 09:00. If you chose **Who**, only that person hears about it; otherwise everyone who may see these records does. Reminders arrive as described in [Notifications](notifications.md). When a repeating duty is done, the reminders move to its next date. Archiving the record or removing the duty stops them.

## Papers

**Papers** are files that belong to a vehicle, pet, home, thing or service: a policy, an invoice, a vet card, a warranty.

- Share the file to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the record. Or, for something already in Quick add, use **File into…** on it.
- Choose a **Kind of paper** (Policy, Invoice, Card, Receipt, Other) when you file it. Press **Edit** on a paper to change the kind or to set **Valid until** if it runs out. Expired papers are marked.
- **Papers filed by hand with Add to… are never sent to the AI.** The AI does not read them, and no suggestion is made. This is the way to keep a sensitive document at home without turning anything off.
- **Detach** removes a paper from the record; it stays in Quick add.

## Archiving

Archiving a vehicle, pet or home hides it, stops its reminders and keeps its papers and history. Duties you no longer need can be removed on their own with **Remove** (tap again to confirm).

## And the AI?

If you share a document without **Add to…**, the AI may suggest marking a duty done or adding an entry; you approve every suggestion. You decide per area how far it may look, and a locked area hides its duties and papers from it: see [Keeping areas away from the AI](ai.md#keeping-areas-away-from-the-ai).
