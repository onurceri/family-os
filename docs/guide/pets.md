# Pets

**Household → Pets** keeps the household's animals in one place: vaccination and parasite-treatment dates, vet details, papers, and a history of what was given and when.

## Add a pet

1. Choose **Add pet** and fill in what you know: a name the family uses ("Luna"), species (dog, cat, bird, rabbit, fish, reptile or other), breed, sex, birth date, microchip number, your vet (clinic and phone, free text), **Who looks after it** and notes. Only the name is needed. A pet photo is not kept yet.
2. Under **What usually needs doing** tick what applies and give the next date for each. The list follows the species (shorter for a fish or a bird):

   | Duty | Repeats | Reminder |
   |---|---|---|
   | Rabies vaccination | every year | 30 days before |
   | Core vaccination booster | every year | 30 days before |
   | Worming (internal parasites) | every three months | 7 days before |
   | Flea and tick treatment | every month | 3 days before |
   | Check-up | every year | 21 days before |

**Good to know**
- These are suggestions; how often a duty comes back (ask your vet) differs by country and family. When you tick one, change the interval (every few months or years, or not at all) and the reminder time.
- Add, change or remove duties later on the pet's page, and add your own (see [What needs doing and Papers](records.md)). Everything is tracked by **date**. **Edit** on the page changes the pet's details.
- You are told ahead of time and on the day.

## Treatments

1. On the pet's page, in **Treatments and vaccinations**, press **Add treatment**.
2. Fill in **Kind** (Vaccination, Internal parasite treatment, External parasite treatment, Check-up, Medication or Other), **Date**, **Product**, **Batch number**, **Vet** and notes.
3. Under **Fulfils** optionally choose the open duty it completes, such as "Rabies vaccination". That duty is marked done and its date moves on by itself.

Link a paper to a treatment with **Paper**. **Remove** (tap again to confirm) deletes a treatment.

## Papers

Share a vet card, a prescription or a receipt to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the pet (or **File into…** on something already in Quick add). It is kept under **Papers** on the pet's page, and **the AI does not read it**.

## What the AI does here

If you share a vet card or receipt without choosing **Add to…**, the AI sees your pets (name, species and breed; not the microchip number, vet or notes) and their open duties. It may suggest adding a treatment to Luna's history and marking the rabies vaccination done, with the next date from the card. You approve each suggestion before anything changes.

To keep pets away from the AI, set **Pets** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**. A pet's duties and papers go with it: the AI sees nothing about your pets, not even a duty such as "Rabies vaccination (Luna)". Everything keeps working by hand.

## Who can do what

- Members who may edit pets change them directly.
- Supervised members see the same buttons and forms, which say "After you save, someone has to approve this.", and their changes wait for approval.
- **Archive pet** (bottom of its page, tap again to confirm) hides the pet and stops its reminders; papers and history are kept.
