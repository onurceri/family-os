# Pets

**Household → Pets** keeps the household's animals in one place: vaccination and parasite-treatment dates, vet details, papers, and a history of what was given and when.

## Add a pet

Choose **Add pet** and fill in what you know: a name the family uses ("Luna"), the species (dog, cat, bird, rabbit, fish, reptile or other), breed, sex, birth date, microchip number, your vet (clinic and phone, as free text), **Who looks after it** and notes. Only the name is needed. A pet photo is not kept yet.

Under **What usually needs doing** tick what applies and give the next date for each. The list follows the species you chose (for a fish or a bird it is shorter):

| Duty | Repeats |
|---|---|
| Rabies vaccination | every year |
| Core vaccination booster | every year |
| Worming (internal parasites) | every three months |
| Flea and tick treatment | every month |
| Check-up | every year |

These are suggestions: how often a duty comes back (ask your vet) differs by country and by family. Each one starts with a usual interval and reminder time, and you can change both when you tick it (every few months or years, or not at all).
You can add, change or remove duties later on the pet's page (see [What needs doing and Papers](records.md)), and add your own. Everything is tracked by **date**. Press **Edit** on the pet's page to change its details.

## Reminders

Each duty is a reminder: you are told ahead of time (30 days for vaccines, 21 for a check-up, 7 for worming and 3 for monthly flea and tick treatment; you can change it) and on the day.

## Treatments

The pet's page has **Treatments and vaccinations**, a history. Press **Add treatment** and fill in the **Kind** (Vaccination, Internal parasite treatment, External parasite treatment, Check-up, Medication or Other), the **Date**, the **Product**, **Batch number**, **Vet** and notes. Under **Fulfils** you can choose which open duty it completes, for example "Rabies vaccination". That duty is then marked done and its date moves on by itself.

## Papers

Share a vet card, a prescription or a receipt to Quick add and choose **Add to…**, then the pet. It is kept under **Papers** on the pet's page, and **the AI does not read it**. For something already in Quick add, use **File into…** on it. You can link a paper to a treatment with **Paper**. **Remove** (tap again to confirm) deletes a treatment.

## What the AI does here

If you share a vet card or receipt without choosing **Add to…**, the AI sees your pets (name, species and breed; not the microchip number, vet or notes) and their open duties, and suggests: add this treatment to Luna's history and mark the rabies vaccination done, with the next date from the card. You approve each suggestion before anything changes.

To keep pets away from the AI, set **Pets** to **Off** under **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**. A pet's duties and papers go with it: the AI then sees nothing about your pets, not even a duty such as "Rabies vaccination (Luna)". Everything keeps working by hand.

## Who can do what

Members who may edit pets change them directly; supervised members see the same buttons and forms, which say "After you save, someone has to approve this.", and their changes wait for approval. **Archive pet** (at the bottom of its page, tap again to confirm) hides the pet and stops its reminders; its papers and history are kept.
