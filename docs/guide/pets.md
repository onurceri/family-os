# Pets

**Household → Pets**: animals with vaccination and parasite-treatment dates, vet details, papers and a treatment history.

## Add a pet

1. **Add pet.** Only the name is required; also: species (dog, cat, bird, rabbit, fish, reptile, other), breed, sex, birth date, microchip number, vet (clinic and phone, free text), **Who looks after it**, notes. No pet photo yet.
2. Under **What usually needs doing** tick duties (the list follows the species; shorter for fish and birds) and give the next date:

   | Duty | Repeats | Reminder |
   |---|---|---|
   | Rabies vaccination | yearly | 30 days before |
   | Core vaccination booster | yearly | 30 days before |
   | Worming (internal parasites) | every 3 months | 7 days before |
   | Flea and tick treatment | monthly | 3 days before |
   | Check-up | yearly | 21 days before |

**Good to know**
- Only suggestions: intervals differ by country and family (ask your vet). Change interval (or none) and reminder when you tick.
- Later, add, change or remove duties on the pet's page ([What needs doing and Papers](records.md)); dates only. **Edit** changes details.
- You are told ahead and on the day.

## Treatments

1. Pet page → **Treatments and vaccinations** → **Add treatment**.
2. **Kind** (Vaccination, Internal parasite treatment, External parasite treatment, Check-up, Medication, Other), **Date**, **Product**, **Batch number**, **Vet**, notes.
3. **Fulfils:** optionally the open duty it completes ("Rabies vaccination"); it is marked done and moves on.

**Paper** links a paper. **Remove** (tap again) deletes a treatment.

## Papers

Share a vet card, prescription or receipt to Quick add, **Add to…**, the pet (or **File into…**). It sits under **Papers**; **the AI does not read it**.

## What the AI does here

For a vet card or receipt shared without **Add to…**, the AI sees pets (name, species, breed; not microchip, vet or notes) and open duties, and may suggest adding a treatment and marking the rabies vaccination done with the card's next date. You approve each.

To exclude pets, set **Pets** to **Off** in **Settings → AI → What the AI may read**; duties and papers go with it, so the AI sees no duty such as "Rabies vaccination (Luna)". Manual use is unchanged.

## Who can do what

- Members who may edit pets change them directly.
- Supervised members get the same forms, marked "After you save, someone has to approve this."; their changes wait.
- **Archive pet** (page bottom, tap again) hides it and stops reminders; papers and history stay.
