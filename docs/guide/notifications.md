# Notifications

Everything is listed in the app; phone notifications and [Telegram](telegram.md) (with approve and reject buttons) are optional copies.

## What you are told about

| Kind | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| Suggestions waiting | People who may approve | A supervised member's share or hand-made suggestion: who and what (**Leo: Change an event: Piano lesson**). One item's suggestions arrive as one, after about 30 seconds. Opens Quick add. |
| Answers to your suggestions | The suggester | Someone else approves or rejects (**Mara said no to your suggestion**). Decided together = one notification. |
| Something you shared was read | The sharer | Shared from outside the app (share sheet, Telegram, email) and the AI has suggestions. Telegram adds buttons. |
| Event reminders | The event's people (all who see the calendar if none) | Timed: 30 min before. All-day: at the [reminder time](region.md) that day (09:00 unless changed). Set per event in **Reminder** ([Calendars](calendars.md)). |
| Task due dates | Assignee (all who see lists if none) | At due time, or the [reminder time](region.md) without one. |
| Chores | Whose turn it is | The [reminder time](region.md) on the due day. Anyone-may-do chores remind nobody ([Chores](chores.md)). |
| Your chores still open today | Whose turn it is, only if switched on | At the times the family sets (18:00 unless changed; up to four a day; **Daily reminder** at the foot of Chores, changed by those who manage chores): how many of yours are due today or late. Off until you switch it on. Opens Chores. |
| Duties | Assignee (all who see records if none) | Days before, and on the due day, at the [reminder time](region.md) ([What needs doing and Papers](records.md)). |
| Backup could not be sent off | Admins | [Backups](backups.md) |
| Auto-approval stopped | Admins | [Auto-approval](auto-approval.md) |
| Updates for extensions you downloaded | Admins | The weekly check finds a newer version ([Extensions](extensions.md)). Opens Extensions. |
| A newer FamilyOS is out | Admins | The weekly check finds a newer FamilyOS, once per version. Opens Extensions, which shows how to update. |

**Good to know**
- Never told about your own suggestions; what you share is yours alone to decide, so other adults are not told about it.
- No answer when you take a suggestion back or auto-approval approves it.
- Quick add page uploads send no "was read" notification.
- Times follow the family time zone ([Region and formats](region.md)).

## The list in the app

- **Computer/tablet:** the **bell** in the navigation shows the unread count; press it for the latest, **Mark all read**, or to open one.
- **Phone:** the bell is on **Today** and opens the full list (also **Settings → You → Notifications**; the number on **More** counts unread).
- Tapping marks read and goes to the thing. **Mark all as read** clears the count.
- A "suggestion to review" leaves the list once decided.
- Notifications are kept 90 days, read or not; older ones leave the list by themselves.
- Each person has their own list, in their language.

## Notifications on a phone or computer

1. **Settings → You → Notifications → Where you get them → This device** (also via **Settings** in the bell popover).
2. Press **Turn on notifications on this device** and allow it in the browser. They arrive even when FamilyOS is closed.
3. **Send a test notification** to check.

**Turn off notifications on this device** affects only this device; the rows under it list your devices with notifications, and **Telegram** shows whether yours is linked.

On a device several people share, signing out turns off the notifications of the person leaving; the next person sees **Turn on notifications on this device** and presses it to receive theirs.

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| iPhone, iPad | Only from the Home Screen app: Safari Share → **Add to Home Screen**, open from there, turn on. iOS 16.4+. |
| Android, Windows, macOS, Linux | Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari. Use the installed app if you have it. |

**Good to know**
- Needs HTTPS ([HTTPS and phones](https.md)).
- If you pressed "Block", allow the site in browser or system settings, then reload.
- A notification already read in the app is not sent to the phone.

## Quiet hours

Switch on **Quiet hours**, set **From** and **Until** (default 22:00–07:00; may cross midnight; family time zone). Nothing goes to phones or Telegram then; the list still fills, and unread ones are sent when quiet hours end.

## What goes where

Under quiet hours the kinds come in three groups, **Day to day**, **Approvals** and **Account and system**: one row per kind, with a switch per channel in a column (**Phone notifications** once a device is on, **Telegram** once linked). Some rows say in a line what the kind is, and link to where it is set up (**Reminder times** for the evening chores count). Choices are yours alone and saved at once. Without a channel the page lists the kinds you get in the app, with no switches, and says how to get them on a phone or in Telegram too. Kinds you can never get (backup message for non-admins) are not listed. Some kinds (**Your chores still open today**, marked **Optional**) are off until you switch one of their channels on, and then arrive in the app too; they need a phone or Telegram channel, so without one they are not listed and do not arrive.
